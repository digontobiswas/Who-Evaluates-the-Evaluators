\section{Experimental setup}
\label{sec:setup}

\paragraph{Policies.}
We use the public NAVSIM baselines---TransFuser (TF)~\cite{chitta2023transfuser} with cameras and
LiDAR, Latent TransFuser (LTF) with cameras only, and the Ego Status MLP without sensors---three
training seeds each, and the public checkpoint of DiffusionDrive (DD)~\cite{liao2025diffusiondrive}, a
diffusion planner on a TransFuser backbone.
All checkpoints reproduce their published navtest PDMS: three-seed means \num{PdmsTF}, \num{PdmsLTF}
and \num{PdmsMLP} against \num{PdmsTFPublished}, \num{PdmsLTFPublished} and \num{PdmsMLPPublished},
and DD \num{PdmsDD} against \num{PdmsDDPublished}.
Degradations are applied to TF and LTF (all six families and the benign families on seed~0; latency and
lateral noise also on seeds~1--2) and to DD (latency, lateral noise and both benign families).
Degradations are \emph{selected} on TF and LTF only; DD is a held-out base on which validated orders
are measured but never chosen.

\paragraph{Evaluators.}
\emph{Open-loop L2} is the average displacement of the plan from the logged trajectory.
\emph{NAVSIM v1} is the PDM Score and \emph{NAVSIM v2} the extended PDM Score in one stage, both on
navtest (\num{NavtestScenarios} scenarios).
We also ran the two-stage pseudo-simulation of NAVSIM v2 on navhard; its synthetic scenes contain no
LiDAR, so TF and DD cannot be scored there, and we report it only where noted.
\emph{BridgeSim}~\cite{zhao2026bridgesim} is our closed-loop reference $E^\star$: NAVSIM scenes
converted to MetaDrive~\cite{li2022metadrive}, run with its leaderboard protocol (log-replay traffic,
pure-pursuit control, replanning every $0.5$\,s, 2\,s warm-up and 8\,s of evaluation).
Its EPDMS column omits ego progress, so a policy that drives less scores higher; we use its driving
score DS (EPDMS without ego progress times route completion) and report EPDMS as a robustness check.
Our BridgeSim runs reproduce the leaderboard DS of LTF (\num{DsLTFNavhard} vs.\ \num{DsLTFPublished}),
TF (\num{DsTFNavhard} vs.\ \num{DsTFPublished}) and DD (\num{DsDDNavhard} vs.\ \num{DsDDPublished}).

\paragraph{Scenes.}
\emph{Selection}: the \num{NavhardScenarios} NavHard scenes of the BridgeSim leaderboard.
\emph{Measurement}: BridgeSim on \num{HeldoutScenarios} navtest scenes that are not in NavHard,
chosen before any of them was simulated as the first converted candidates in a fixed SHA-256 order of
their tokens (one further scene failed in the simulator for every policy and is excluded for all);
NAVSIM and L2 on the \num{NavtestHeldout} navtest scenes outside NavHard.
The \num{SharedHeldout} BridgeSim measurement scenes are also navtest scenes, so all per-scene
comparisons between evaluators use exactly the same scenes.

\paragraph{Implementation.}
All evaluators run unmodified at pinned commits; policies are wrapped, never retrained.
Each run writes per-scenario scores; every number in this paper is computed from those files with
$B=1000$ bootstrap resamples and generated into the text by script.
