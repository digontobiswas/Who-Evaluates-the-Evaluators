\section{Experimental setup}
\label{sec:setup}

\paragraph{Policies.}
We use the public NAVSIM baselines: TransFuser~\cite{chitta2023transfuser} (cameras and LiDAR),
Latent TransFuser (LTF, cameras only) and the Ego Status MLP (no sensors), three training seeds each.
Degradations are applied to TransFuser and LTF, seed~0 for all families and seeds~1--2 for latency
and lateral noise (replication).

\paragraph{Evaluators and scenarios.}
\emph{Open-loop L2} is the average displacement of the plan from the logged trajectory on navtest.
\emph{NAVSIM v1} is the PDM Score on navtest (\num{NavtestScenarios} scenarios).
\emph{NAVSIM v2} is the extended PDM Score in one stage on navtest, and the two-stage
pseudo-simulation on navhard (combined score); its synthetic scenes contain no LiDAR, so TransFuser
cannot be evaluated there.
\emph{BridgeSim}~\cite{zhao2026bridgesim} is our closed-loop reference $E^\star$: the \num{NavhardScenarios}
NavHard scenarios converted to MetaDrive~\cite{li2022metadrive}, run with the leaderboard protocol
(log-replay traffic, pure-pursuit control, replanning every $0.5$\,s, 8\,s of evaluation).
Its EPDMS column omits ego progress, so a policy that drives less scores higher; we use its driving
score DS (EPDMS without ego progress times route completion) and report EPDMS as a sensitivity check.
\num{SharedScenarios} NavHard scenarios are also navtest scenarios; all cross-evaluator comparisons
of individual scenarios use this shared set.

\paragraph{Implementation.}
All evaluators run unmodified at pinned commits; policies are wrapped, never retrained.
Each run writes per-scenario scores; every number in this paper is computed from those files with
$B=1000$ bootstrap resamples.
